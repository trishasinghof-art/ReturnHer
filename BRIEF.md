# ReturnHer Brief v2

## What ReturnHer is (every screen must make this clear in 5 seconds)
ReturnHer helps women across India find government-supported ways to study, get certified and earn, matched to her education, skills, goal and district. It is a personalised pathway, not a job portal and not a list of schemes.

## Personality
Bold, joyful, warm, kind. Think of a festival morning in a courtyard: marigolds, rangoli, a sewing machine humming, chai on the stove, and nobody judging. It should feel made by a woman, for a woman: handcrafted, generous, a little playful; never corporate, never techy, never a government form.
Golden rule: LOUD PICTURES, QUIET TEXT. Illustrations and colour blocks are big and vibrant. All reading text sits on calm cream, oat or marigold with high contrast and large size.

## Typography (two families only)
Headings: Fraunces (Google Fonts variable, with opsz, wght, SOFT and WONK axes), weight 800, SOFT 100, line-height 1.05.
Body: Nunito Sans 400/600/700.
Hindi: Noto Serif Devanagari 700 for headings, Noto Sans Devanagari for body, line-height 1.6.
Sizes: hero h1 clamp(44px, 7vw, 84px); section h2 clamp(34px, 4.5vw, 56px); body 18px / 1.65; buttons 18px bold; nothing below 15px. No all-caps shouting. Highlight one word per heading with a hand-drawn underline or circle (inline SVG).

## Layout rules (so it never looks like a template)
1. Nothing sits in a plain rectangle. Mix shapes: arch-top, 28px rounded, pill, leaf (two opposite corners heavily rounded), circle, scalloped edge.
2. Overlap: cards overlap neighbours and the section above or below by 24 to 60px. Most cards have a flat offset colour block behind them.
3. Bleed: illustrations break out of their boxes: heads above the top edge, arms and props over the sides, figures crossing a section boundary. Use overflow visible, negative margins, z-index.
4. Asymmetry: staggered columns (alternate translateY), 7/5 splits, slight rotation (-3 to +3 degrees) on cards that straightens on hover. Only the hero text is centred.
5. Section edges are wavy, scalloped or arched SVG dividers, never a straight line.
6. Section background order on the home page: forest, cream, oat, blush, forest, cream, marigold, deep-forest.
7. No shadows and no gradients in the UI. Depth comes only from overlapping flat colour blocks.
8. Mobile keeps overlap and bleed at reduced amounts. No horizontal scroll ever (overflow-x: clip on body). Body text must never sit on busy artwork.

## Illustration rules
Flat vector, bold simple shapes, 5 to 6 palette colours per scene. Women are the main characters, of different ages (20s to 60s), skin tones, body types, hair (braid, bun, short, curls, grey, long), clothes (saree, salwar kameez, kurta with jeans, dupatta, hijab, cardigan) and abilities (include a cane, a hearing aid, a wheelchair user). Faces are simple: dot or closed eyes, small smile arc, optional bindi or glasses. Adult and dignified, never childlike.
Show women learning, making, teaching, researching, selling, leading and laughing together, not only domestic work. Never show a woman crying, afraid, pitied, chained, dependent on a man, or with a man looming over her. No stock photos.
Props: sewing machine, needle and spool, books, laptop, notebook, chai cup, certificate scroll, scissors, graduation cap, lab flask.

## Decorative motifs (Indian-rooted, not "tech")
marigold flowers, paisley (kairi), lotus, stacked bangles, rangoli dot clusters, squiggles, hatch lines, dupatta waves, sun with rays, leaves, small birds.
Do NOT use the 4-point sparkle star anywhere, and no robot, magic-wand or glow icons. They read as AI.

## Motion (slow and gentle)
Fade-up on scroll, figures sway slowly (6s), flowers rotate (40s), cards straighten on hover, journey lines draw themselves, saving something makes a marigold bloom with petals. All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Language and dignity
Never use: dropout, unemployed, housewife, backward, weak, failed, only, "just a". Use: paused, returning, restarting, continuing, next chapter. No urgency pressure, no red warnings, no countdown timers. Voice: warm, direct, short sentences, a little humour, plain words.

## Privacy and honesty
Never ask for Aadhaar, phone number or exact address. Only first name, qualification, skills, goal, state, district. Always say: "No Aadhaar. No phone number. Your answers stay on this device."
Show on every page a slim notice: "Prototype for demonstration. Opportunity entries are samples. Please confirm details on the official portals." Never claim to be an official government website; say "built on official government sources".
